OmLeaf Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

OmLeaf is a breathing-practice timer by Charlie Pradeep. It is designed to work entirely on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. OmLeaf has no account, no ads and no analytics, and the app does not request internet access. We do not collect, receive or share any personal data.

What is stored on your phone

Your sound settings, any breathing patterns you create, and a simple history of the practice sessions you complete (date, practice name, length and rounds) are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them. You can clear the history at any time in Settings.

Permissions

OmLeaf asks for permission to vibrate the phone (optional haptic taps). It does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or storage.

Sound

All tones are generated on your phone while you practise. Nothing is recorded.

Health note

OmLeaf is a timer and guide, not medical advice. Do not strain, and stop if you feel dizzy or unwell. Check with your doctor before practising if you have a medical condition or are pregnant.

Children

OmLeaf does not collect data from anyone, including children.
