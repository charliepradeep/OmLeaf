**Full description**
```
OmLeaf is a calm, private timer for pranayama and breath-led chanting.

PRACTICES
• Deep belly breathing, equal breathing, box breathing and 4-7-8
• Ujjayi, Bhramari (humming bee), Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril)
• Chandra Bhedana, Surya Bhedana, Sheetali
• Kapalabhati and Bhastrika, with safety notes
• AUM / OM chanting and A-sound chanting

BUILD YOUR OWN
Set breathe-in, hold, breathe-out and hold times, the number of rounds and the rest between them. Save your patterns.

CHANTING WITH A GUIDE TONE
Choose AUM, A, U, M or humming. A guide tone follows your chant, and AUM is split in the traditional 1:1:2 (A, U, then a long M). Pick a pitch such as 136.1 Hz, 261.6 Hz or 432 Hz, or set any value from 80 to 600 Hz.

SEE AND FEEL THE BREATH
A breathing circle grows and shrinks with each breath. Optional soft chimes and gentle vibration mark each change. The nostril to use is shown for alternate-nostril practices.

TRACK YOUR PRACTICE
See your streak, your total minutes and the last seven days.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics. The app has no internet permission and makes every sound on your phone.

OmLeaf is a timer and guide, not medical advice. Do not strain, and check with your doctor first if you have a medical condition or are pregnant.
```

## Declarations
- **Data safety:** no data collected, no data shared. No data is transmitted (the app has no internet permission). Data is not required to be deleted because none leaves the device.
- **Ads:** none. **App access:** all functionality is available without login.
- **Content rating:** answer honestly; there is no user-generated content, violence or gambling.
- **Target audience:** adults (18+) is the simplest choice.
- **Health apps declaration:** Google may ask you to complete this because of the category. Answer that the app gives general wellness guidance, not diagnosis or treatment, and keep the in-app disclaimers.
- **Trader status:** the account-level declaration you already made applies. A free app with no earnings is a strong case for non-trader, but you decide.
